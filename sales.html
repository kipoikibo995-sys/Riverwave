Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any video editing experience?

No. There is no timeline and no keyframes. You paste your text, pick a style and press Render. The editor walks you through five simple steps, and the live preview shows exactly what you will get.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time payment of $17. There are no monthly fees for making videos.

What do I need to use it?

A computer with Chrome or Edge. The app runs in your browser and renders videos on your own computer, so there is nothing to install. Keep the tab open while a video renders; longer multi-page videos take a little longer.

Which platforms are the videos for?

Videos are vertical 9:16 in Full HD (1080 × 1920), the format used by YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels. In Chrome and Edge they save as MP4. You can also save any page as a PNG image for Pinterest or Instagram posts.

Can I use the quotes from the library commercially?

Yes. The original pieces in the library were written for Quote Video Studio and may be used freely in your videos, including commercially. The classics come from public-domain works published before 1929.

Does the voiceover cost extra?

No. Free voices run directly on your computer, and premium voices are included with no account or API key needed. Premium voice generation has a fair-use hourly limit.

Can I add my own music?

Yes. Upload an MP3, WAV or M4A file. It fades in and out, loops if needed and gets quieter while the voice speaks. Please use music you have the rights to.

Does it work for any genre or language?

It works for any genre: romance, fantasy, thriller, poetry, self-help, non-fiction. Text can be in any language your chosen font supports. The voiceover voices are English.
